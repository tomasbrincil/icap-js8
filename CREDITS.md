# Credits

This project is a small addition on top of a great deal of other people's work.
None of the people or projects below has reviewed or endorsed it; mistakes in it
are ours alone. Names are given as their own projects state them.

## The work this app is derived from

**Dawid SQ6EMM** - the custom firmware for the IC-705 with the ICAP app loader,
the ICAP SDK, and the FT8 reference app. This project exists only because of
them: our decoder is a fork of his FT8 decoder (`apps/ft8/ft8dec.c`), our app
shell reuses the capture loop of his `digiapp.c` and the two-slot scheduling of
his `wsprapp.c`, `digi_util.c/.h` are his files
unmodified, and the app is built with his SDK header, start-up code, link script
and packer.
<https://github.com/sq6emm/icap-sdk> ·
<https://sq6emm.github.io/icom-custom-firmware/>

**Jordan Sherer KN4CRD** - creator of JS8 and JS8Call. The protocol, the frame
formats, the Huffman table, the directed commands and group calls, and the
262144-word JSC dictionary are his work ("(C) 2018 Jordan Sherer"). Our message
layer re-implements the logic of his `varicode.cpp` and `jsc.cpp`; the dictionary
files for the SD card are generated from his `jsc_list.cpp`.
<https://github.com/js8call/js8call>

**The JS8Call and JS8Call-improved contributors** - among them **Allan Bazinet
W6BAZ**, whose C++ port of the JS8 encoder and decoder (`JS8.cpp`, "(C) 2025 Allan
Bazinet") we read next to the original Fortran, and the JS8Call-improved team,
who maintain that fork and publish the off-air test recordings we used. Where
JS8Call and JS8Call-improved differ in the handling of the dictionary's `size`
field, we follow JS8Call-improved.
<https://github.com/JS8Call-improved/JS8Call-improved>
JS8Call's own About dialog thanks KC9QNE, KI6SSI, K0OG, LB9YH, M0IAX, N0JDS,
OH8STN, VA3OSO, VK1MIC, W0FW and W6BAZ.

**The WSJT-X development team** - Joe Taylor K1JT, Bill Somerville G4WJS, Steve
Franke K9AN, Nico Palermo IV3NWV, Uwe Risse DG2YCB, Brian Moran N9ADG and the
contributors they acknowledge. JS8 stands on FT8: the LDPC (174,87) code, the
Costas synchronisation, the 8-FSK frame and the decoding approach come from
WSJT-X, of which JS8Call is a derivative.
<https://wsjt.sourceforge.io/>

**Kārlis Goba YL3JG** - ft8_lib, the compact FT8 library written for
resource-constrained devices. The candidate search, the sync score and the
belief-propagation decoder in the SDK's FT8 decoder, and therefore in ours, are
adapted from it.
<https://github.com/kgoba/ft8_lib>

**BI1BIF** - found the entry points of the IC-705's own on-screen keyboard in the
stock firmware and published his analysis and an emulator of the radio. Writing a
message on the radio itself, the heart of release 0.2.0, rests on that work: the app
calls the three functions he identified (`shell/js8app_kbd.h`, `NOTICE.md` section 2.1a).
<https://github.com/BI1BIF/IC-705-Firmware>

## Test recordings (used for testing, not redistributed)

The receiver was tested against recordings made and published by others. They are
**not** part of this repository. The tests run without them, and accept
recordings of your own through `RECORDINGS=<dir>` (`docs/BUILDING.md`).

| Source | What | Terms stated by the source |
|---|---|---|
| JS8Call-improved, `media/tests/` | off-air JS8 recordings, one period each, used by that project's own decoder test | repository licence GPL-3.0 |
| GitHub user **nickr01**, `js8call-tests`, `inputs/` | test audio for JS8Call QA (off-air material and files assembled in an audio editor) | no licence stated in the repository; for that reason we do not redistribute the files |
| GitHub user **randal007** ("Randal"), `x6100-js8`, `test-audio/js8_test.wav` | synthetic test band generated by that project's own JS8 encoder | repository LICENSE is LGPL-2.1 |
| Signal Identification Wiki, `File:JS8-10-27-2020.wav`, uploaded by user **HamRadioOperator73** | JS8Call audio recorded from a cassette tape | "Public Domain CC0" (file page) |

Thank you for publishing them. Real names or callsigns of these authors are given
only where the source itself states them.
